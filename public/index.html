Regional University Lab Apprenticeship

State Universities & Regional Research Institutes

Over 68% of high school ISEF finalists find mentors by respectfully cold-emailing junior faculty, postdocs, and PhD students at regional colleges near their home.

Grades 9-12 Dry Lab / Computational Self-Directed
01

Offer Free Assistance, Never Ask For Pay

University grant funding is strictly tied to enrolled graduate students. State clearly that you are seeking knowledge and experience with zero financial expectation.

02

Target Junior Faculty, Postdocs & PhDs

Tenured chairs get 200+ emails/day. Postdoctoral researchers and senior PhD candidates do the hands-on coding and welcome diligent high school assistants to speed up data pipelines.

03

Focus on Computational / "Dry Lab" Skills

OSHA and university safety regulations heavily restrict minors in bio/chem wet-labs. Computational tasks (Python, PyTorch, R, data scraping) can be performed remotely and legally.

04

Show Tangible Proof of Work

Don't just say you love science: link a clean GitHub repo, Kaggle analysis, or a 1-page literature review note proving you read and understood their recent publication.
